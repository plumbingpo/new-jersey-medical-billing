CareCloud

CareCloud offers a comprehensive suite of healthcare technology solutions, including medical billing services, practice management software, and EHR systems, designed to streamline operations and improve financial performance for medical practices.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Specialty Care (e.g., Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics), Urgent Care, Mental Health, Ambulatory Surgery Centers.

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 4-7%), per-claim options may be available for specific services.

  • ✅ Integrated practice management and EHR system for a unified platform.
  • ✅ Strong focus on analytics and reporting to identify revenue cycle opportunities.
  • ⚠️ Can be a more premium-priced solution, potentially less suitable for very small practices.
3

AdvantEdge Healthcare Solutions

AdvantEdge Healthcare Solutions provides outsourced medical billing and practice management services, leveraging advanced technology and expert billers to optimize revenue cycles and ensure compliance for healthcare providers.

Specialties Served: Anesthesia, Pathology, Radiology, Emergency Medicine, Hospital-Based Physician Groups, Ambulatory Surgery Centers.

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 3-6%), customized based on volume and specialty.

  • ✅ Deep expertise in complex, hospital-based and specialty billing.
  • ✅ Dedicated account managers and robust reporting.
  • ⚠️ May be less flexible for practices seeking only a basic billing service without full practice management integration.

Coronis Health

Coronis Health is a large national revenue cycle management company that offers specialized medical billing services across various healthcare sectors, utilizing a combination of technology and experienced personnel to maximize collections.

Specialties Served: Anesthesia, Radiology, Pathology, Laboratory, EMS, Behavioral Health, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Gastroenterology.

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 3-7%), varies by specialty and service level.

  • ✅ Extensive experience across a wide range of specialties, including highly complex areas.
  • ✅ Focus on leveraging technology for efficiency and accuracy.
  • ⚠️ Being a large company, smaller practices might experience less personalized attention compared to boutique firms.
5

Medical Billing Wholesalers (MBW)

Medical Billing Wholesalers offers comprehensive medical billing and coding services, acting as an outsourced partner to help practices reduce administrative burden, improve cash flow, and ensure compliance with healthcare regulations.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Dermatology, Gastroenterology, Mental Health, Urgent Care.

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 3-6%), can also offer per-claim or hourly rates for specific tasks.

  • ✅ Cost-effective solutions due to offshore capabilities.
  • ✅ Strong emphasis on reducing denials and improving collection rates.
  • ⚠️ Communication can sometimes be a challenge due to time zone differences and cultural nuances.
6

eClinicalWorks RCM (Revenue Cycle Management)

eClinicalWorks RCM provides integrated billing services directly linked with their popular EHR system, offering a seamless workflow from patient encounter to payment processing, designed for practices already using or considering their EHR.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Cardiology, Dermatology, Gastroenterology, Urgent Care.

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 5-8%), often bundled with EHR subscriptions or as an add-on service.

  • ✅ Deep integration with the eClinicalWorks EHR, streamlining workflows.
  • ✅ Single vendor for both EHR and billing, simplifying support.
  • ⚠️ Primarily beneficial for practices already using or committed to the eClinicalWorks EHR; less suitable for others.
7

PMR Healthcare

PMR Healthcare specializes in medical billing and practice management for independent physician practices, focusing on personalized service to optimize revenue, reduce administrative overhead, and improve overall practice efficiency.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Physical Therapy, Mental Health.

Pricing Model: Percentage of collections (estimated 4-7%), with flexible plans and potential for flat fees for certain services.

  • ✅ Strong focus on independent practices and personalized service.
  • ✅ Proactive with denial management and appeal processes.
  • ⚠️ May have a smaller operational scale compared to national providers, potentially limiting resources for very large, multi-specialty groups.
